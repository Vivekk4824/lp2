Department Overview

Established in 2001, the Department of Information Technology is dedicated to excellence in technical education, research, and industry collaboration. Our curriculum bridges core computer science fundamentals with modern software engineering practices.

We offer an undergraduate B.Tech in Information Technology program accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). Students gain hands-on exposure to Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Full-Stack Development.

Our Vision

To emerge as a globally recognized center of excellence in Information Technology education and applied research, producing ethical and industry-ready technocrats.

Our Mission

  • M1: Impart strong theoretical foundations and practical problem-solving skills in computing.
  • M2: Foster an ecosystem of innovation, open-source contribution, and entrepreneurship.
  • M3: Collaborate with leading IT industries to keep curricula aligned with emerging technologies.
